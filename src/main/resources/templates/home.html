Open a savings or current account and manage it online

Apply with one form. Your 16-digit card number and 4-digit PIN are issued as soon as you submit it. After that you can deposit, withdraw, transfer to other accounts and read your statement.

Open an account Sign in

This is a demonstration project. Balances are not real money and no real money is held or moved. Use test values instead of your own PAN and Aadhaar numbers.

What you can do

Deposit

Add money to your account. Every deposit appears in your statement with the balance after it.

Withdraw

Take money out after confirming with your PIN. A withdrawal is refused if the balance does not cover it.

Transfer

Send money to another account using its card number. Both statements are updated together or not at all.

Statement

Every transaction in date order with the running balance. Print it straight from your browser.

Before you apply

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Have your PAN and Aadhaar number ready. Your PAN is stored encrypted and only the last four digits of your Aadhaar number are kept.
  • Each deposit, withdrawal or transfer is limited to limit.
  • Read how your details are handled in the privacy policy.
